How to Play

Click the battlefield to activate mouse look. Move the mouse left/right and up/down to aim. Use W S to move forward and backward and A D to strafe.

The firearm now points toward the crosshair. Shots can strike the head, torso, arms, or legs. Head shots deal more damage; limb shots deal less and briefly slow the target.

Your own British eliminations earn 10 coins. Coins remain saved and can purchase cosmetic uniforms from the main-menu Uniform Store.

Click to fire and press R to reload. A large reload timer and progress bar appear near the center of the screen. Running, vaulting and big hits interrupt the reload.

Press 1 for your rank weapon, 2 for a rock, 3 for your melee weapon, and 4 for the camp axe. Press E near supplies to gather them.

Press Tab for crafting, V to build a wall, F to build a tiny fort, and C to vault a low wall. CPU allies and enemies route around walls.

Higher ranks move faster, reload sooner, have less shot spread, and maintain effective fire at longer distances. Health remains 100 for every rank.

Historical note: rank did not rigidly assign one unique firearm in every army. This game uses plausible period equipment to create distinct progression: line soldiers use muskets, sergeants and some officers use lighter fusils, cavalry roles use carbines and pistols, and officers carry swords.

Story mode contains five normal battlefield rounds. Round six is the commander battle: a larger British force versus you and four CPU-controlled American Privates. The commander has no boss-only stat bonus.
